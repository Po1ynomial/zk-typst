# Zettelkasten archive

This context is a personal archive of connected thoughts. These terms describe its knowledge and relationships, independent of storage, commands, and editor integration.

## Language

### Archive

A self-contained collection of Zettel, their connections, and associated material belonging to one person.

_Avoid_: vault, workspace when referring to the archive itself.

### Zettel

An independently addressable note that develops one principal thought. A Zettel may express a claim, question, argument, concept, or observation.

_Avoid_: document as a synonym for Zettel; not every document is a Zettel.

### Note ID

The stable address of a Zettel, independent of its title and subject.

_Avoid_: title, category, creation time as synonyms for identity.

### Reference

An authored occurrence that points to another Zettel by its note ID.

_Avoid_: link when the distinction between one occurrence and the relationship matters.

### Link

A directed relationship from one Zettel to another, represented by one or more references in the source Zettel.

_Avoid_: reference when describing the grouped relationship rather than an occurrence.

### Backlink

An incoming link viewed from its target Zettel. It does not imply a reciprocal link from the target to the source.

### Link context

The prose explaining why a referenced Zettel matters to the thought being developed.

### Structure note

A Zettel whose principal thought is an arrangement of other thoughts and their relationships.

_Avoid_: folder, category, special node type as synonyms for structure note.

### Metadata field

An archive-selected attribute of a Zettel used to describe or retrieve it. Its name does not imply a universal classification or role.

### Metadata occurrence

An authored use of a metadata field in a Zettel. Several occurrences may concern the same field.

_Avoid_: declaration as a synonym for an occurrence.

### Asset

An ordinary file associated with a Zettel that supplies supporting material, such as an image or a source fragment. Association does not imply exclusive ownership by that Zettel.
